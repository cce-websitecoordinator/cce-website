Message

Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), Irinjalakuda has emerged as one of the fastest-growing engineering institutions in Kerala. Established in 2015, the college has achieved NBA accreditation for its eligible programmes and Autonomous Status within just ten years of its inception—a remarkable milestone that reflects its commitment to academic excellence and quality.

The institution distinguishes itself through its Placement Training Programmes and Student Enrichment Programmes (SEPs), which systematically prepare students with the technical, professional, and interpersonal skills demanded by industry. The success of these initiatives is evident from the outstanding placement record. As a recent testimony, three of our current final-year students have each secured placement offers of ₹33 lakh per annum, highlighting the quality of our students and the effectiveness of our training ecosystem.

Our greatest strength is our highly committed faculty. Beyond delivering quality education, our teachers actively mentor students, conduct placement training, and lead SEPs, contributing significantly to the institution's consistently high pass percentages and excellent placement performance, making Christ College of Engineering one of the leading engineering institutions in Kerala.
The college also fosters a strong research and innovation culture. Faculty members are engaged in meaningful research, particularly in the interdisciplinary field of Engineering for Medical Science, through undergraduate and postgraduate projects, doctoral research, and collaborative initiatives. Our steadily increasing number of research publications, patents, and innovations reflects this commitment to creating knowledge and developing engineering solutions that benefit society.
By integrating academic excellence, industry readiness, research, innovation, and values-based education, Christ College of Engineering continues to produce competent, socially responsible, and globally employable engineers.
