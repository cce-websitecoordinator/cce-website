About

Functions of the R&D Cell:

  • Monitor and oversee all research activities in the institution.
  • Coordinate research programs and projects.
  • Manage resources efficiently for research activities.
  • Review and ensure research quality and standards.
  • Guide researchers on publication and ethical practices.
  • Facilitate training and skill development for researchers.
  • Promote collaboration with industry, government, and other institutions.
  • Assist researchers in obtaining funding and grants.
  • Ensure safety and good laboratory practices in research labs.
  • Act as a liaison between researchers and funding agencies.

Objectives of the R&D Cell:

  • Create a strong research environment in the institution.
  • Encourage high-quality and impactful research.
  • Foster collaboration with industry and government bodies.
  • Provide resources and support for innovative research.
  • Promote ethical standards and good practices in research.
  • Support researchers in publishing their work in recognized journals.
  • Facilitate resource mobilization and funding for research projects.
  • Build the research capacity of faculty and students.
  • Develop and implement research policies and strategies.
  • Contribute to national goals like self-reliance through research.
