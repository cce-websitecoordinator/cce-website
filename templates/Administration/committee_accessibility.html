Committee for Accessibility Standards and Inclusive Practices

Objectives

  • Ensure the Accessibility Guidelines and Standards for HEIs and Universities from UGC is followed in the Institution.
  • Ensure inclusive campus life for all students.
  • Ensure all buildings and infrastructure facilities are accessible and disabled‑friendly for all.

Committee Constituting

To ensure the Accessibility Guidelines and Standards for HEIs and Universities from UGC is followed in the Institution, a Committee for Accessibility Standards and Inclusive Practices is constituted.
