Anti-Ragging Committee

(As per UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009)

To ensure a ragging-free campus environment by preventing and addressing incidents of ragging in compliance with UGC Regulations and relevant legal provisions, thereby ensuring the physical and emotional well-being of all students, especially newcomers.

Functions of the Anti-Ragging Committee

  • Policy Implementation:
    • Ensure strict implementation of UGC Regulations.
    • Frame and revise anti-ragging policies and code of conduct.
  • Prevention Measures:
    • Create awareness through seminars, workshops, orientation programs, and posters.
    • Ensure anti-ragging affidavits are submitted by students and parents.
  • Campus Surveillance:
    • Maintain vigilance in hostels, canteens, classrooms, and other areas.
    • Conduct regular inspections by faculty or staff.
  • Complaint Handling:
    • Receive and investigate complaints (written, oral, anonymous).
    • Forward serious cases to the Anti-Ragging Squad.
  • Disciplinary Action:
    • Recommend actions like suspension, expulsion, or legal proceedings.
  • Coordination and Reporting:
    • Act as the nodal body for UGC's Anti-Ragging Helpline/portal.
    • Submit regular reports to UGC and affiliating university.
  • Support for Victims:
    • Provide counseling and psychological support.
    • Ensure confidentiality and protection of complainants.

All students are advised to strictly adhere to the anti-ragging guidelines. Any violation will invite strict disciplinary and legal action.

Ragging in any form is a punishable offense and is strictly prohibited on and off campus.
