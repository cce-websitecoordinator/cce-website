Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

Objectives

  • Create a safe working environment for women.
  • Redress complaints in a time-bound, fair, and confidential manner.
  • Sensitize employees regarding POSH Act provisions.
  • Ensure legal compliance with prevention protocols.
  • Recommend disciplinary actions based on inquiry findings.

Key Functions

  • Register complaints via internalcomplaint@cce.edu.in.
  • Conduct impartial inquiries within 90 days.
  • Maintain strict confidentiality throughout the process.
  • Submit reports with findings to the employer within 10 days.
  • Present annual status reports to Management.
