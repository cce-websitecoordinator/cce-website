Health Facilities at Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous)

At Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), the well-being of our students is a top priority. We are committed to fostering a supportive and healthy campus environment through a range of essential health facilities.

Common Rooms

Boys’ Common Room

A dedicated space for male students to relax, take short breaks, or attend to minor health needs in a private setting.

Girls’ Common Room

A separate, well-equipped area for female students offering privacy, rest, and basic amenities for comfort during the day.

Doctor Consultation Facility

We provide on-campus medical consultation to address the health concerns of students and staff.

Counseling Services

Recognizing the importance of mental health, our professional counseling services support students in coping with academic, personal, or emotional challenges.

Students can schedule confidential sessions with our trained counselor for guidance, emotional support, and mental well-being.
