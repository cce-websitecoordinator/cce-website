CNC Machining Centre

About

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Christ College of Engineering houses a state-of-the-art CNC Machining Centre dedicated to advancing manufacturing education and precision engineering research. This facility provides students and researchers with hands-on access to industry-grade equipment for learning advanced CNC programming, machining operations, and manufacturing process optimization. The centre supports academic coursework, project-based learning, and collaborative research with industry partners.
