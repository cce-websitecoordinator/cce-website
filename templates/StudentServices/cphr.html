Center for Pneumatic and Hydraulic Research (CPHR)

About

The Center for Pneumatic and Hydraulic Research (CPHR) is a dedicated research and training division under Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous). It aims to advance education and innovation in the fields of industrial pneumatics, medical pneumatics, and fluid power systems.

CPHR serves as a unique platform where “Engineering Meets Medical Innovation”, promoting interdisciplinary learning and real-world applications. The center is equipped with advanced pneumatic trainers and dental equipment simulators, enabling hands-on experience and industry-relevant skill development.

Key Objectives of CPHR

  • Offer certification programs in General and Dental Pneumatic Technology (GDPT)
  • Facilitate research projects, workshops, and industrial collaborations
  • Provide technical consultancy and product development support
  • Train students and professionals in smart pneumatics, automation, and medical equipment servicing

Why Choose GDPT @ CPHR – Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous)?

  • Industry-aligned curriculum with practical applications
  • Access to advanced pneumatic lab and dental equipment
  • Expert trainers from engineering and healthcare domains
  • Certification with high employment value in both general and medical fields
  • Gateway to careers in medical device servicing, dental clinics, automation industries, and technical sales/support

“Shape your future in the world of intelligent pneumatics and medical technology only @ Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), Irinjalakuda.”

Courses Offered

  1. Six-Month Certification Course
  2. Hands-on training program on dental equipment for BDS, MDS, and dental practitioners
